# Competition Control Feedback and Support Portal Acceptable Use Policy

_Last updated: October 14, 2026_

This Acceptable Use Policy ("Policy") covers the Competition Control feedback and support
portal at quack.recf.org (also reachable at quack.recf.cloud), including its feedback boards,
roadmap, changelog, help center and documentation, support messenger and support tickets,
status page, and the feedback widget that the portal provides to other RECF websites and
applications (collectively, the "Portal") provided by the Robotics Education & Competition
Foundation ("RECF", "we", "us"). It is designed to protect RECF, event organizers,
competition participants, and the general Internet community from unethical, irresponsible,
and illegal activity. By accessing or using the Portal, you agree to be bound by this Policy.

This Policy is incorporated into the Competition Control Feedback and Support Portal Terms and
Conditions (the "Terms") by reference. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings
given in the Terms. Use of the Competition Control website and cloud services is governed by
the Competition Control Acceptable Use Policy published at
<https://c2.recf.org/legal/acceptable-use-policy>, and use of the Competition Control
application installed on your own computers is governed by the Competition Control End User
License Agreement ("EULA").

If you — or anyone using the Portal through you — are found to be engaging in activities
prohibited by this Policy your access to the Portal may be suspended and your account may be
terminated. In extreme cases, RECF may be legally obliged to report such activity to the
relevant authorities.

## Fair use

The Portal is provided free of charge to support RECF competitions and the people who run
them. We provide it with the expectation that your use will be consistent with submitting and
discussing feedback about, obtaining support for, and staying informed about Competition
Control and related RECF programs. If your use is considered excessive or places an
unreasonable load on the Portal, RECF may restrict capacity or limit your access.

We are opposed to all forms of abuse, discrimination, rights infringement, and any action that
harms or disadvantages any group, individual, or resource. We expect users to engage the
Portal with the same intent.

## User accountability

You are responsible for your own actions and for the actions of anyone who uses the Portal
with your permission or through your account. This responsibility also applies to anyone who
uses the Portal on an unauthorized basis as a result of your failure to put in place
reasonable security measures, such as safeguarding your account credentials.

By using the Portal, you agree to ensure adherence to this Policy by anyone using it through
your account or with your permission. Complaints regarding the actions of a user will be
forwarded to the contact for the account in question.

If you — or anyone using the Portal through you — violate this Policy, RECF reserves the
right to suspend or terminate your access to the Portal or your account, and to take any
remedial or preventative action it deems appropriate, without notice.

## Prohibited activity

### Infringing and unlawful material

The Portal may not be used to transmit, distribute, or store any material in violation of
applicable law. This includes but is not limited to:

- any material protected by copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual
  property right used without proper authorization; and
- any material that is obscene, defamatory, constitutes an illegal threat, or violates export
  control laws.

You are solely responsible for all material you input, upload, transmit, create, or publish
through the Portal — including feedback posts, comments, support messages, attachments, and
images — and for obtaining legal permission to use any works included in that material.

### Personal information and data misuse

The Portal is used by people who work with competition participants, many of whom are minors,
and with coaches and volunteers. You may not:

- post or transmit personal information about students or other minors, or about any other
  person without their permission, including names, contact details, images, or event data
  (such as team, coach, or inspection information) beyond what is necessary to describe your
  feedback or support request;
- collect, harvest, or compile personal information from the Portal, including the names and
  activity of other users, for marketing, profiling, resale, or any other purpose unrelated
  to the Portal;
- provide personal information to RECF without having obtained the consents, notices, and
  authorizations required under applicable child privacy, educational privacy, and data
  protection laws; or
- otherwise violate any person's privacy.

### Unauthorized access and interference

The Portal may not be used to:

- access any account, board, conversation, or electronic resource that you do not own or are
  not authorized to access, including by circumventing account sign-in, private boards, the
  signed identity used by the feedback widget, or other access controls or security features;
- intentionally or recklessly introduce viruses or malicious code into the Portal or into any
  connected system;
- access the Portal by automated means other than through interfaces RECF makes available
  for that purpose, or otherwise scrape, probe, or place unreasonable load on it;
- initiate network sniffing, packet spoofing, flooding, denial-of-service attacks, or similar
  activity against the Portal or against any other user;
- interfere with the operation of the Portal or with any other user's use of it;
- create or use multiple accounts, false identities, or automated means to manipulate votes,
  rankings, or discussions, or to evade a suspension or moderation decision;
- advertise, transmit, or make available any software, program, product, or service designed
  to violate this Policy or the acceptable use policy of any other service provider; or
- use RECF's content obtained through the Portal, including documentation, help center
  articles, and changelog entries, to train or improve machine learning or artificial
  intelligence systems without RECF's prior written consent. This does not restrict your
  rights under the open source licenses described in Section 8 of the Terms.

### Harassment and harmful content

The Portal may not be used to engage in activities designed to harass another group or
individual. Harassment includes but is not limited to hate speech, advocacy of racial or
ethnic intolerance, and any activity intended to threaten, abuse, infringe upon the rights
of, or discriminate against any group or individual. You may not publish or transmit through
the Portal any content or links that incite violence, depict a violent act, depict the sexual
exploitation of minors, or threaten anyone's health or safety.

### Unsolicited messages

The Portal may not be used to send unsolicited bulk or commercial messages, including
promotional or off-topic posts and comments, to build or run unconfirmed mailing or telephone
lists, or to collect email addresses or telephone numbers of other users, RECF staff, or
volunteers for those purposes.

### Other unlawful or unethical activity

The Portal may not be used for any act or omission in violation of consumer protection laws
and regulations, or by any person or entity involved with or suspected of involvement in
illegal gambling; terrorism; narcotics trafficking; arms trafficking; or the proliferation,
development, design, manufacture, production, stockpiling, or use of nuclear, chemical, or
biological weapons, weapons of mass destruction, or missiles, including any affiliation with
others who support such activities or causes.

### Unauthorized use of RECF property

You may not impersonate RECF, RECF staff, or volunteers, misrepresent a business relationship
with RECF, or claim ownership of any RECF property — including the Portal's content and
RECF's names, logos, and brands — for the purpose of fraudulently gaining service, patronage,
or user trust. You may not present a post, comment, or other communication as official or
sanctioned by RECF when it is not.

### AI features

You may not use the AI features of the Portal to:

- attempt to bypass, disable, or manipulate their safeguards or instructions, including
  through "jailbreak" or prompt-injection techniques, or to extract their underlying
  instructions or configuration;
- generate content that violates this Policy, including harassing, hateful, sexually
  explicit, or unlawful content, or content about a specific student or other minor;
- present AI Output (as defined in the Terms and Conditions) as an official statement,
  position, rule interpretation, or ruling of RECF, or as the work of a person when it is
  not; or
- submit large volumes of automated requests or otherwise use them in a way that degrades
  their availability for others.

## Enforcement

RECF may investigate suspected violations of this Policy and may, without notice, remove,
hold, or disable access to content, suspend or terminate accounts, or take other remedial or
preventative action it deems appropriate. RECF may also cooperate with law enforcement and
other authorities, and disclose information as described in the Privacy Policy, in
connection with suspected unlawful activity. Because the Portal is provided free of charge,
no credit or compensation is available for interruptions resulting from a violation of this
Policy.

## About this policy

This Policy outlines a non-exclusive list of activities and intent RECF deems unacceptable
and incompatible with the Portal. Changes to this Policy are made as described in Section 17
of the Terms; the revised version will be posted at
<https://quack.recf.org/legal/acceptable-use-policy>. Questions about this Policy may be sent
to support@recf.org.
