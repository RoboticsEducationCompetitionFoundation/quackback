# Competition Control Acceptable Use Policy

_Last updated: September 23, 2026_

This Acceptable Use Policy ("Policy") covers the Competition Control website and download
portal, the Competition Control documentation site, the Competition Control cloud services,
the Competition Control feedback portal, and the public results API (collectively, the "Site
and Services") provided by the Robotics
Education & Competition Foundation ("RECF", "we", "us"). It is designed to protect RECF, event
organizers, competition participants, and the general Internet community from unethical,
irresponsible, and illegal activity. By accessing or using the Site and Services, you agree
to be bound by this Policy.

This Policy is incorporated into the Competition Control Website and Cloud Services Terms and
Conditions (the "Terms") by reference. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings
given in the Terms. Use of the Competition Control application installed on your own
computers is governed by the Competition Control End User License Agreement ("EULA"),
including the Authorized User Terms of Use in its Annex 1.

If you — or anyone using the Site and Services through you — are found to be engaging in
activities prohibited by this Policy your access to the Site and Services may be suspended
and your account may be terminated. In extreme cases, RECF may be legally obliged to report
such activity to the relevant authorities.

## Fair use

The Site and Services are provided free of charge to support RECF competitions. We provide
them with the expectation that your use will be consistent with organizing, running,
supporting, and following robotics competition events. If your use is considered excessive
or places an unreasonable load on the Site and Services, RECF may restrict capacity or limit
your access.

We are opposed to all forms of abuse, discrimination, rights infringement, and any action that
harms or disadvantages any group, individual, or resource. We expect users to engage the Site
and Services with the same intent.

## User accountability

You are responsible for your own actions and for the actions of anyone who uses the Site and
Services with your permission — including volunteers and other individuals to whom you grant
access as an event organizer. This responsibility also applies to anyone who uses the Site
and Services on an unauthorized basis as a result of your failure to put in place reasonable
security measures, such as safeguarding your account credentials and securing the host
computer, client devices, and local network used to run an event.

By using the Site and Services, you agree to ensure adherence to this Policy by anyone using
them through your account or with your permission. Complaints regarding the actions of a user
or the individuals they have authorized will be forwarded to the contact for the account in
question.

If you — or anyone using the Site and Services through you — violate this Policy, RECF
reserves the right to suspend or terminate your access to the Site and Services or your
account, and to take any remedial or preventative action it deems appropriate, without
notice. Access granted to volunteers or other individuals by an event organizer may likewise
be revoked at any time by the organizer or by RECF without notice.

## Prohibited activity

### Infringing and unlawful material

The Site and Services may not be used to transmit, distribute, or store any material in
violation of applicable law. This includes but is not limited to:

- any material protected by copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual
  property right used without proper authorization; and
- any material that is obscene, defamatory, constitutes an illegal threat, or violates export
  control laws.

You are solely responsible for all material you input, upload, transmit, create, or publish
through the Site and Services — including team names, award names, event descriptions,
announcements, and other content displayed to event audiences — and for obtaining legal
permission to use any works included in that material.

### Personal information and data misuse

The Site and Services process information about competition participants, many of whom are
minors, and about coaches and volunteers. You may not:

- access, use, or disclose event data or RECF Events data for any purpose other than
  organizing, running, and following the applicable competition event;
- collect, harvest, or compile personal information from the Site and Services, including
  through the public results API, for marketing, profiling, resale, or any other purpose
  unrelated to the competition;
- provide personal information to RECF without having obtained the consents, notices, and
  authorizations required under applicable child privacy, educational privacy, and data
  protection laws; or
- otherwise violate any person's privacy.

### Unauthorized access and interference

The Site and Services may not be used to:

- access any account, device, event, or electronic resource that you do not own or are not
  authorized to access, including by circumventing release gating, device authorization,
  account sign-in, or other access controls or security features;
- intentionally or recklessly introduce viruses or malicious code into the Site and Services
  or into any connected system;
- access the Site and Services by automated means other than through the public results API,
  or otherwise scrape, probe, or place unreasonable load on them;
- initiate network sniffing, packet spoofing, flooding, denial-of-service attacks, or similar
  activity against the Site and Services or against any other user;
- interfere with the operation of the Site and Services or with any other user's use of them;
- alter, falsify, or destroy competition results, rankings, inspection records, or other
  event data, or attempt to do so, except through the Software's intended scoring and
  administration functions by an authorized user;
- advertise, transmit, or make available any software, program, product, or service designed
  to violate this Policy or the acceptable use policy of any other service provider; or
- use the Software, Documentation, Event Data, RECF Events Data, API output, or any content
  obtained through the Site or Services to train or improve machine learning or artificial
  intelligence systems without RECF's prior written consent.

### Harassment and harmful content

The Site and Services may not be used to engage in activities designed to harass another
group or individual. Harassment includes but is not limited to hate speech, advocacy of
racial or ethnic intolerance, and any activity intended to threaten, abuse, infringe upon the
rights of, or discriminate against any group or individual. You may not publish or transmit
through the Site and Services — including on audience displays — any content or links that
incite violence, depict a violent act, depict the sexual exploitation of minors, or threaten
anyone's health or safety.

### Unsolicited messages

The Site and Services may not be used to send unsolicited bulk or commercial messages, to
build or run unconfirmed mailing or telephone lists, or to collect email addresses or
telephone numbers — including coach and volunteer contact information made available for
running an event — for those purposes.

### Other unlawful or unethical activity

The Site and Services may not be used for any act or omission in violation of consumer
protection laws and regulations, or by any person or entity involved with or suspected of
involvement in illegal gambling; terrorism; narcotics trafficking; arms trafficking; or the
proliferation, development, design, manufacture, production, stockpiling, or use of nuclear,
chemical, or biological weapons, weapons of mass destruction, or missiles, including any
affiliation with others who support such activities or causes.

### Unauthorized use of RECF property

You may not impersonate RECF, misrepresent a business relationship with RECF, or claim
ownership of any RECF property — including the Site and Services, the Software, and RECF's
names, logos, and brands — for the purpose of fraudulently gaining service, patronage, or
user trust. You may not present an event, results, or communications as official or
sanctioned by RECF when they are not.

## Enforcement

RECF may investigate suspected violations of this Policy and may, without notice, remove or
disable access to content, suspend or terminate accounts or device authorizations, or take
other remedial or preventative action it deems appropriate. RECF may also cooperate with law
enforcement and other authorities, and disclose information as described in the Privacy
Policy, in connection with suspected unlawful activity. Because the Site and Services are
provided free of charge, no credit or compensation is available for interruptions resulting
from a violation of this Policy.

## About this policy

This Policy outlines a non-exclusive list of activities and intent RECF deems unacceptable
and incompatible with the Site and Services. Changes to this Policy are made as described in
Section 17 of the Terms; the revised version will be posted at
<https://c2.recf.org/legal/acceptable-use-policy>. Questions about this Policy may be sent to
support@recf.org.
