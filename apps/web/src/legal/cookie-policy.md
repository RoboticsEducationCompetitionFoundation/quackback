# Competition Control Feedback Portal Cookie Policy

_Last updated: September 23, 2026_

This Cookie Policy explains how the Robotics Education & Competition Foundation ("RECF", "we",
"us", or "our") uses cookies, browser storage, scripts, and similar technologies (collectively,
"Cookies") when you visit the Competition Control feedback portal at quack.recf.cloud, including
its feedback boards, roadmap, changelog, help center, administration pages, and the feedback
widget it provides to other RECF websites and applications (the "Site").

This Cookie Policy does not cover the Competition Control website at c2.recf.org, which has its
own Cookie Policy, or the Competition Control application and the devices connected to an
event's host computer; the browser storage they use is described under "Device and browser
storage" in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).

This Cookie Policy should be read together with our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy) and
[Terms and Conditions](/legal/terms-of-service).

## 1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Cookies allow a
website to recognize a browser, remember preferences, improve functionality, and deliver
customized content.

Similar technologies include browser local storage and session storage, and scripts or embedded
content loaded from another site. References to "Cookies" in this Policy include these related
technologies.

## 2. Why we use Cookies

We use Cookies to:

- Operate and secure the Site;
- Authenticate users and maintain sessions;
- Remember your Cookie choices and display preferences;
- Provide the feedback widget;
- Understand how the Site is used, where you have consented to it; and
- Troubleshoot technical issues.

## 3. Types of Cookies we use

### A. Strictly necessary Cookies

These Cookies are required for the operation of the Site and cannot be disabled through our
Cookie Settings. They are used to:

- Maintain sign-in sessions and authenticate users when you submit feedback, vote, comment, or
  use the administration pages;
- Enable security features; and
- Remember your Cookie choices.

Without these Cookies, certain features of the Site may not function properly.

### B. Functional Cookies

Functional Cookies allow us to provide enhanced features and remember choices you make. On the
Site, these Cookies remember your light or dark appearance setting and your help center
language, and let the feedback widget remember your session and which updates you have seen.

If disabled, some features may not operate as intended.

### C. Analytics Cookies

With your permission, we use first-party analytics to understand how the Site is used, such as
which pages are visited and how visitors arrive at them. These analytics are operated by RECF on
the Site itself; no third-party analytics provider receives this information. Analytics Cookies
are not placed, and no page visits are recorded, until you allow them through the Cookie banner.

### D. Advertising and social media Cookies

We do not currently use advertising or social media Cookies on the Site. If we introduce them,
we will update this Cookie Policy and, where required by law, ask for your consent before
placing them.

## 4. First-party and third-party Cookies

**First-party Cookies** are placed directly by the Site and are used to provide the Site and
improve your experience. All of the Cookies listed above are first-party Cookies.

**Third-party Cookies** are placed by another site. When the feedback widget is embedded in
another website, it runs from quack.recf.cloud and its Cookies belong to the Site rather than to
the website it appears on; that website's own Cookie policy governs its Cookies. Signing in may be
handled by Microsoft Entra ID, which may set Cookies on Microsoft's sites under Microsoft's own
privacy policy. We do not control Microsoft's Cookie practices.

## 5. Information collected through Cookies

Depending on the technology used, Cookies may collect:

- IP address;
- Browser type and version;
- Operating system;
- Language preferences;
- Session information;
- Referring URLs;
- Pages viewed; and
- Other technical information relating to your interaction with the Site.

## 6. Your Cookie choices

You may manage Cookie preferences through:

### Browser settings

Most browsers allow you to:

- Block Cookies;
- Delete existing Cookies;
- Restrict certain categories of Cookies; and
- Receive notifications before Cookies are stored.

Please note that disabling certain Cookies may affect Site functionality.

### Cookie Settings

When you first visit the Site, a banner asks whether you allow non-essential Cookies. You can
change your choice at any time with the **Cookie Settings** link at the bottom of every page.

Changes to preferences may not remove previously stored Cookies but will affect future Cookie
activity.

## 7. Global Privacy Control and Do-Not-Track signals

Certain jurisdictions recognize browser-based preference signals, such as the Global Privacy
Control (GPC).

Where required by applicable law, we will honor valid opt-out preference signals associated with
your browser or device.

Because there is not yet a universally accepted standard for all "Do Not Track" signals, the
Site may not respond to every DNT mechanism.

## 8. California privacy rights

Residents of California may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as
amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).

Subject to applicable law, California residents may have the right to:

- Know what personal information has been collected;
- Request deletion of personal information;
- Request correction of inaccurate information;
- Access personal information;
- Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information;
- Limit the use of sensitive personal information where applicable; and
- Exercise rights without discrimination.

To exercise these rights, please see our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy) or contact us
at support@recf.org or at the mailing address in the Contact section below.

## 9. European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland

If you are located in the European Economic Area ("EEA"), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we
rely upon appropriate legal bases before placing non-essential Cookies.

Non-essential Cookies are not placed until you provide consent through the Cookie banner.

You may withdraw consent at any time through Cookie Settings.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing conducted before consent was
withdrawn.

## 10. Data retention

Cookies may remain on your device for different periods of time:

**Session Cookies** expire when you close your browser.

**Persistent Cookies** remain on your device until they expire or are deleted.

Retention periods vary depending on the purpose of each Cookie and applicable legal
requirements. The durations of the Cookies we use are listed in the table in Section 3.

## 11. International data transfers

Information collected through Cookies may be processed in countries other than the country in
which you reside.

Where required by law, we implement appropriate safeguards for cross-border data transfers,
including:

- Standard Contractual Clauses;
- Data Processing Agreements;
- Security controls; and
- Other legally recognized transfer mechanisms.

## 12. Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time.

When material changes are made, we may provide notice through the Site, by email, or through
other reasonable means.

The "Last updated" date above reflects the effective date of the current version.
